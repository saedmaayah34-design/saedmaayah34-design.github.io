Simple Portfolio Website

1. Open index.html in a browser.
2. Replace YOUR_SID in index.html with your student ID.
3. Replace YOUR_UNIVERSITY_EMAIL with your university email.
4. Put your own formal photo in this folder and name it my-photo.jpg.
5. Use the Home and Contact Me links to navigate.
6. The form validates fields and shows alerts, but does not send messages to a server.
